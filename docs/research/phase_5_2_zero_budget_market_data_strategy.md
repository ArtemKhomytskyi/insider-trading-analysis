# Phase 5.2 — strict $0 market-data reassessment

Checked 2026-10-16. Research only: no market-data request or download was made. This supersedes the cost recommendations in the earlier provider comparison. A public endpoint or a free Python package is **not** itself a data license. Rights below refer to the published terms found, not a legal opinion or a guarantee that a provider will remain free forever.

## Decision

**NO-GO for the original 2006–2026, full-US-market, bias-controlled price study at $0.** I found no source with verified permanent free access to that coverage *and* explicit permission for automated acquisition, persistent local storage, and publication of non-reconstructable research results. The defensible $0 route is a **frozen, restricted-universe 2006–2017 study**, conditional on provenance and row-level coverage checks of an openly labeled archive. Report only results for securities/dates with independently verified identity and observed prices; do not call this a complete US-insider universe or extrapolate to 2018–2026.

### Free, but license or fitness remains unclear

- [Stooq](https://stooq.com/) exposes historical downloads, but I could not establish an authoritative license for automated bulk collection, persistent storage, adjusted-price semantics, delisted completeness, or derived publication. **Do not ingest automatically** on the assumption that a CSV URL is permission.
- Other community/Kaggle archives may carry open-looking license labels, but their upstream provenance, adjustment methods, survivorship properties and latest dates must be checked individually. A CC0 label alone cannot grant rights the uploader does not own.

### Technically accessible, but prohibited or unsuitable for this project

- **Yahoo through yfinance:** [yfinance](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/blob/main/README.md) describes personal-use access and is unaffiliated with Yahoo; [Yahoo's EU-facing terms](https://legal.yahoo.com/ie/de/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html) prohibit automated collection without prior express permission. The existing pilot code does not confer that permission. **NO-GO for unattended price acquisition.**
- **Tiingo Starter:** although the [free plan](https://app.tiingo.com/pricing/) advertises historical equities and quotas, the current [terms §1.6(a)](https://app.tiingo.com/tos/) forbid persistent storage of both raw data **and derived products** on Starter; the project requires a durable SQLite evidence trail. **NO-GO**; do not use it even as a validation oracle, as §1.6(c) also restricts using Tiingo to validate another dataset.
- **Alpha Vantage free:** [documentation](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/) limits free daily prices to the latest 100 observations; full daily history and daily-adjusted data are outside the free entitlement. Its free historical listing-status file (from 2010) can help identity QA, not the required price history.
- **EODHD free:** [free entitlement](https://eodhd.com/pricing) is 20 API calls/day and roughly one year of ordinary historical access (apart from demo symbols); insufficient for 2006–2026 research.
- **Massive free:** [free plan](https://massive.com/pricing?product=stocks) has a two-year historical window and five calls/min; its bars are [split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/is-massives-stock-data-adjusted-for-splits-or-dividends). Insufficient for the intended interval.
- **Twelve Data free:** its [terms](https://twelvedata.com/terms) allow internal storage/derived calculations subject to tier limits but restrict redistribution and external display; a published research use is not clearly authorized, and exchange/data-provider terms can add restrictions. Do not assume its API coverage implies a publication license.

## Defensible restricted-universe design

1. Freeze an **ex ante** sample from SEC insider transactions dated within a window such as **2006-01-01 through 2017-10-31**; exclude events whose outcome horizon extends beyond the archive's 2017-11-10 end. The window begins only where each selected security's observed series actually begins. Do **not** select names by today's listing status or by archive availability after seeing returns.
2. Join by the project's historical security ID, issuer CIK, share class and a **dated, independently evidenced ticker interval**. Price-file ticker strings are candidates, never identity proof. Quarantine reused tickers, mergers, symbol/name changes, dual classes and ambiguous predecessor/successor series.
3. Define a transparent coverage funnel: eligible SEC events → independently mapped events → archive ticker present → sufficient pre/post-event bars → corporate-action review → analyzable events. Publish counts and exclusions by year, industry and transaction type. Preserve a missing-price indicator; never convert missing/delisted returns to zero or drop them silently.
4. Because the CC0 archive's OHLC fields are **already adjusted for dividends and splits**, treat it as an *adjusted-series-only* return source, not as raw execution prices. Independently document material actions from SEC/issuer records; if adjustment factors cannot be reconciled, reject affected event windows. Delisting proceeds, cash mergers, spinoffs and bankruptcy terminal values require separate evidence; otherwise censor and disclose the bias. Report close-to-close or other explicitly specified adjusted-series returns, not total-return precision beyond the evidence.
5. Use a frozen archive version, checksum, source URL, retrieval date, and source/rights manifest. Keep it in a **separate research/pilot database**. Never silently merge its bars with a newer feed or production SQLite. If provenance or usage rights cannot be established to the owner's standard, stop before acquisition/publication.

The result is an **archive-covered historical subset**, not a representative 2006–2026 US market panel. Key biases: source survivorship/listing selection, historical ticker reuse, missing delisted and terminal returns, unknown dividend/split adjustment algorithm, corporate-action look-ahead/back-adjustment, unobserved trading days/volume, and selection induced by excluding ambiguous mappings. With a frozen 2017 endpoint there is no basis for claims about 2018–2026. Present effect sizes alongside the coverage funnel and sensitivity bounds for unresolved/missing outcomes.

## First acquisition plan and gates

**No download is approved by this report.** First run an offline rights-and-schema preflight: record the archive's exact CC0 statement, version/date and any upstream-source disclosure; list expected fields, period, asset types, and transaction/security IDs for a small *preselected* cohort; write synthetic tests for ticker reuse, missing bars, action windows, and delisting censoring. Ask the owner to choose whether CC0-uploader provenance is acceptable for an exploratory, noncommercial study; if not, do not acquire the archive. Once approved, make **one official Kaggle archive retrieval** (no scraping or parallel polling), checksum it, extract to a quarantine directory, inspect a small fixed sample, and ingest only validated rows to a new pilot database. Respect Kaggle's current API/access terms at acquisition time.

**GO only if**: the owner accepts the archive's documented rights/provenance risk; the cohort is fixed before price inspection; each row passes CIK/share-class/dated-ticker evidence; file schema/date bounds and adjustments are verified; corporate actions and delisted/terminal outcomes are either evidenced or explicitly censored; coverage/exclusion statistics are reported; no raw data are redistributed unless rights are independently confirmed.

**NO-GO if**: the goal remains a complete 2006–2026 market, provenance is inadequate, automated/storage/publication rights are uncertain for the chosen source, ticker-only joins are required, or missing/delisted returns would be silently excluded. SEC identity work can continue at $0 even while price acquisition is held.

**Smallest next implementation task:** add an **offline, provider-agnostic source-license/coverage manifest plus fail-closed validator** to the isolated Phase 5.2 pilot. It should accept a frozen archive path, recorded license/provenance/checksum/date range/field semantics, and preapproved historical IDs; reject missing evidence, out-of-interval prices and unknown adjustment/action status. Use synthetic CSV fixtures only. Do not change the production downloader or database.
